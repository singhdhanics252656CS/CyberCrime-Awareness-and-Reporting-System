    Habits that protect you

    • Use a long phrase of four or more random words. Length beats clever symbols.
    • Use a different password for every important account.
    • Turn on two-step verification for email, banking and social media.
    • Never share an OTP, PIN or CVV with anyone, whoever they say they are.
